# HUD preview scenes

The coast, sunlit painted village and rain-lit city are original AI-generated images created for PlxNative. They depict fictional scenes and do not use frames, characters, logos or artwork from existing films. Generation prompts and source hashes are recorded in scenes.json.

The native PlxNative simulator renders the actual HUD, chapter thumbnails and information panels over synthetic media derived from these images. All titles, chapters and episode data in these previews are fictional.

HUD layouts retain their package licenses; the app fonts retain the licenses shipped with the pinned native SDK.
